# UrbanFlow

UrbanFlow is a **Python-first microscopic city-traffic simulator and research workbench**. It serves reinforcement-learning signal control, transportation research and teaching.

- **Deterministic engine.** Vectorised NumPy engine with individual vehicles, IDM car-following, MOBIL lane changing and conflict-aware intersections.
- **Signal control.** Fixed-time, actuated, max-pressure, Webster, external/RL and emergency preemption.
- **Research APIs.** Gymnasium and PettingZoo environments, experiments with statistical comparison, and reproducible benchmarks.
- **Replays and workbench.** Compact, seekable replays and a web workbench for live simulation, replay, scenario editing and experiment dashboards.

!!! note "Status"
    UrbanFlow is under active development. It is built in vertical slices, and these pages grow with each one.
